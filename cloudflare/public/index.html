How Spotted finds the moment

  1. Add your videos. Pick them in Google Photos or upload MP4 and MOV files. Each one is copied, analysed and searchable in about a minute.
  2. Spotted watches them. It samples the frames that change, describes each one, transcribes what is said and tags objects, places and sounds.
  3. Search in plain words. Ask for “the goal celebration” or “an ambulance”. You get the frame, the timestamp and a short answer that cites it.

Questions

What is Spotted?

Spotted is an AI video search app for your own videos. You describe a moment, such as a scene, an object or something said, and it finds the exact frame and timestamp, with a short answer that cites where it saw it.

How do I add videos?

Sign in with Google, then pick videos in Google Photos with Google's own picker, or upload MP4 or MOV files. Spotted only sees the videos you pick.

Can other people see my videos?

No. Every person has a private library. Nobody else can see, search or play your videos, and you can delete any video at any time.

Is Spotted free?

Yes. Each account can add 20 videos (up to 500 MB) and run 30 searches a day, free.

How does the search work?

Spotted samples frames from each video and describes them with Google Gemini, transcribes speech with Whisper, then combines vector search, a reranker and a label hierarchy, so a football match does not match “birthday party”.
