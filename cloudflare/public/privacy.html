Privacy policy

Your videos,
your data.

Last updated 16 October 2026

Spotted lets you search your own videos by what happens in them. This page explains what Spotted collects, who processes it, how long it is kept and how to delete it.

Who runs Spotted

Spotted is operated by Chirag Goel. Questions about this policy or your data: connect.chirag.g@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your Google account basics: your name, email address and profile picture, received when you sign in with Google. They identify your account and appear in the account menu.
  • Videos you add: files you upload, and videos you pick in Google Photos.
  • What is derived from them: key frames, captions and summaries, transcripts of speech, and tags, which make your videos searchable.
  • Usage counts: how many videos and how much storage you use, and how many searches you run each day, so that limits can be applied.

Who processes it

  • Google Gemini describes frames, writes captions and summaries, and composes answers to your searches.
  • Cloudflare runs and stores Spotted: Workers (the app), R2 (video and frame storage), D1 (the database), Vectorize (the search index) and Workers AI (including Whisper, for transcripts).

These providers process data only to provide Spotted's features.

Google Photos access

When you connect Google Photos, Spotted asks for read-only access, and only to the items you pick in the Google Photos picker. It cannot see the rest of your library, and it never changes or deletes anything in Google Photos. You can remove Spotted's access at any time in your Google account permissions.

Spotted's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How long we keep it

  • Original videos are deleted automatically 7 days after they are added.
  • Frames and derived text (captions, transcripts, tags and the search index) are kept until you delete the video, so search keeps working.

How to delete your data

  • One video: open it in Spotted and choose Delete. The video, its frames and all derived text are removed.
  • Your whole account: email connect.chirag.g@gmail.com to delete your account, and everything in it will be removed.

No selling, no ads

Spotted does not sell your data, does not show ads and does not use your data for advertising.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes too.
